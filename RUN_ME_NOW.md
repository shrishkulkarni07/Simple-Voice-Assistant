## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### If microphone doesn't work:
1. Check microphone is plugged in
2. Windows Settings → Privacy → Microphone → Allow desktop apps
3. Speak AFTER you see "Ready! Listening..."

### If you hear nothing:
- Check speaker volume
- Speaker might be muted
