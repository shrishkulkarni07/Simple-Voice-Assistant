HOW IT WORKS (Explain this to your teacher):
=====================================================

1. You speak into microphone
2. Windows Audio System captures sound (NO PyAudio!)
3. SpeechRecognition library records audio
4. Audio sent to Google Cloud API (via internet)
5. Google's AI converts voice to text
6. Python code processes the text
7. Performs action (tell time, open website, etc.)
8. pyttsx3 converts response to speech
9. You hear the answer!
